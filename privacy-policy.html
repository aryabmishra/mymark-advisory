LEGAL & COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy

Transparency, confidentiality, and data protection are fundamental to our advisory partnerships.

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

1 Introduction & Commitment

Welcome to MyMark Advisory LLC ("MyMark Advisory," "we," "us," or "our"), founded and led by Tiffany Tucker. MyMark Advisory provides executive strategic advisory, leadership coaching, keynote speaking, organizational alignment, and youth mentorship programs across the United States.

We respect the privacy of every client, partner, organization, and website visitor. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, safeguard, and manage personal and organizational information when you interact with our website (mymarkadvisory.com), schedule advisory consultations, or communicate with our team.

Core Commitment: We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or organizational strategic data to third-party data brokers or marketing lists.

2 Information We Collect

We collect information in the following circumstances to deliver tailored advisory and consulting services:

  • Consultation & Inquiries: When you schedule a consultation via our live Calendly booking integration or submit an inquiry via email (yes@mymark.pro), we collect your name, corporate/organization email address, phone number, organization name, role/title, and any strategic context or objectives you share.
  • Advisory & Engagement Data: In the course of executive consulting, leadership development workshops, or keynote coordination, we may receive business context, operational challenges, and strategic goals necessary to fulfill the advisory scope.
  • Technical & Log Data: When you visit our website, our hosting and network infrastructure (powered by Cloudflare) automatically logs standard technical metadata such as your internet protocol (IP) address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and timestamps. This data is used solely for site performance, security monitoring, and fraud prevention.
  • Cookies & Local Storage: We use minimal, essential cookies and browser session storage strictly necessary to operate the site, maintain security, and load embedded appointment scheduling tools. We do not use intrusive cross-site tracking cookies.

3 How We Use Your Information

We process collected information for legitimate professional purposes, including:

  • Coordinating, scheduling, and conducting executive consultations and discovery calls with Founder Tiffany Tucker;
  • Developing customized strategic roadmaps, leadership frameworks, and workshop curricula for client organizations;
  • Communicating administrative updates, scheduling confirmations, proposals, or direct responses to inquiries;
  • Ensuring technical stability, uptime, and security across our digital platforms;
  • Fulfilling legal, contractual, and regulatory compliance obligations.

4 Confidentiality & Client Non-Disclosure

Strategic advisory requires deep institutional trust. All non-public organizational insights, business metrics, operational structures, and proprietary leadership deliberations shared with Tiffany Tucker or MyMark Advisory LLC are treated with strict confidentiality.

We routinely execute mutual non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) with corporate, educational, and civic clients before commencing formal advisory engagements. Client discussions, notes, and strategic documents are stored in secure, access-controlled environments.

5 Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website and delivering client services. These providers have access to your data only as necessary to perform designated services on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality standards:

  • Calendly LLC: Powers our online consultation booking calendar and scheduling automation. Calendly processes appointment times, names, and contact details according to their security and privacy standards.
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: Provides web hosting, content delivery network (CDN), SSL encryption, and cyber protection against automated attacks.
  • Google Workspace: Used for secure administrative email communications, calendar synchronization, and document collaboration.

6 Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard technical, administrative, and physical safeguards to protect personal and business information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. All web traffic between your browser and our website is protected using Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL) encryption.

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, service client accounts, resolve disputes, and comply with legal requirements. When data is no longer needed, it is securely destroyed or anonymized.

7 Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access & Review: Request a summary of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction & Updates: Request correction of inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete details.
  • Deletion & Erasure: Request the deletion of your personal contact data, subject to legal and contractual retention requirements.
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe or opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us directly at yes@mymark.pro. We respond to verified requests promptly.

8 Youth Mentorship & Educational Programs

MyMark Advisory LLC proudly conducts youth mentorship and empowerment initiatives. All youth programs, student leadership workshops, and educational engagements are coordinated directly with accredited schools, non-profit institutions, community centers, or verified legal guardians.

Our website is directed at business professionals, institutional leaders, and adults seeking advisory services. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information online from children under the age of 13. If you believe a minor has submitted personal information through this website, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

9 Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our operational practices, new service offerings, or evolving legal frameworks. When revisions occur, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document will be updated accordingly. Continued use of our website or services following any update indicates acceptance of the revised terms.
