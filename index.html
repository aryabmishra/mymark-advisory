THE PERSON BEHIND THE PURPOSE

Meet Tiffany Tucker. Founder & CEO, MyMark Advisory LLC

Behind every meaningful change is a leader who bridges sectors, challenges conventional thinking, and turns vision into measurable outcomes. As CEO of MyMark Advisory LLC, Tiffany Tucker is a results-oriented strategic partnerships executive with over twenty years of experience architecting high-impact alliances across public, private, and nonprofit sectors—transforming complex, multi-million dollar initiatives into sustainable, scalable models.

Tiffany currently serves as Program Executive in the Strategic Partnerships Division at the New York City Department of Youth and Community Development (DYCD), directing high-value project portfolios and engineering data-informed systems that advanced partnership ROI by 90%. She previously served as Deputy Chief Service Officer for Community Engagement at NYC Service within the Mayor’s Office, and Deputy Director of Community Partnerships at the New York County District Attorney’s Office—driving high-level government relations, institutional trust, and systemic operational efficiency.

A dedicated public servant, educator, and community advocate, Tiffany is the founder of two nonprofits—Redemption, Inc., which has served over 10,000 youth, and Stop Hiding, Inc.—and has long championed volunteer initiatives supporting organizations like the Hope Empowerment and Development Zone (HEADZ). Her honors include the 1010 WINS Tomorrow’s Newsmakers Award for Public Service. Tiffany holds Master’s degrees from New York University and Seton Hall University, an Executive Leadership Certificate in Collaborative Solutions from Harvard University’s Kennedy School, and a BA from Adelphi University—while proudly considering being a mother to her two sons among her greatest accomplishments.
